Scientists working in the mysterious Area 51 were experimenting with different virus strains when one day an experiment 
went wrong and caused a large explosion destroying almost the entire facility. The explosion transformed everyone in the
immediate vicinity into the living dead, zombies thirsty for blood who quickly rampaged through America, killing and
turning people into zombies. You are a soldier who was sent on a mission to cull the zombie threat that went wrong and
ended up leaving your squad dead. With everyone around you either dead or turned, and the zombie horde slowly moving in, you have one goal. 
Survive.
